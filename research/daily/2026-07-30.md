*   **Laravel v13.23.0 Release:** The Laravel team released v13.23.0 on July 27, 2026, introducing a `monthly` log driver, SES tenant support, and PostgreSQL `using()` clause for migrations.

*   **Fact:** Voting is active for PHP 8.6 deprecations. The `list()` removal remains the most contested item. [Source: PHP Internals]

## Engineering Decisions

*   **Adopt `monthly` Log Driver:** Use for smaller applications to avoid directory bloat associated with the `daily` driver.
*   **Utilize `->using()` in Migrations:** Mandatory for PostgreSQL type casts to avoid migration failures during schema changes.
*   **Centralize Queue Routing:** Use class-based routing in Laravel 13 to avoid hardcoding connection strings in jobs.
