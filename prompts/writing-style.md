# ✍️ Writing Style Guidelines

This document outlines the strict voice and writing rules for AI agents generating content for Abdulrahman Hussin.

---

## 🎭 Tone Matrix
* **Voice**: Active, direct, educational, builder-to-builder.
* **Humor**: Dry, self-aware engineering humor (e.g., debugging loops, premature optimization, developer vs. marketer viewpoints).
* **Authority**: Pragmatic Expert. Speak from implementation, database logs, and actual business metrics.

---

## 🚫 Things to Avoid (Anti-Phrases)
AI agents must **never** use the following words or phrases:
* *"In today's fast-paced digital world / landscape..."*
* *"Unleash the power of..."*
* *"Revolutionize your workflow..."*
* *"As a passionate developer..."*
* *"Delve", "testament", "pivotal", "furthermore"*
* Emojis placed inside code snippets.

---

## 📏 Formatting Rules
1. **Sentence Structure**: Clear, short-to-medium sentences. Active voice only.
2. **Visual Hierarchy**: Use code blocks for SQL/PHP/Go snippets, bold headers, and structured bullet lists.
3. **No Fluff**: Skip generic greeting hooks or boilerplate introductions. Jump straight into the conflict, code, or design decision.
4. **CTAs**: Keep call-to-actions conversational and soft (e.g., *"How do you handle connection pools in your setup? Let's discuss below."*).
